2 · Seller or carrier? Decomposing the delay

For every late order, dispatch time is compared with that seller's own median, and transit time with the median for that seller-state → customer-state lane. "Abnormal" = more than × the baseline.

Method caveat (read before quoting these numbers)

This is a heuristic attribution, not a causal estimate: we never observe what transit would have been without the seller's delay. Baselines need ≥30 orders; thinner sellers/lanes fall back to the seller-state / customer-state median (). The "seller-first" rule in the original brief labels an order seller_delay whenever dispatch is abnormal even if transit is also abnormal, which is why the 4-way split is shown.
